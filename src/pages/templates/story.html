The Genesis

HONORING THE DARK SACRAMENT

Ganachly wasn't conceived inside a venture-backed boardroom or an industrial commissary. It was forged in the quiet after-hours of Faridabad. By day, Hardik navigated the rigid demands of a full-time corporate profession; by twilight, the kitchen came alive. Every single batch was, and remains, mixed and baked strictly after 6:30 PM.

The name draws reverence from Ganache—that velvet alchemy of dark chocolate and fresh cream that serves as the beating pulse of traditional French patisserie. If the emulsion is rushed, it separates; if compromised with synthetic fillers, it goes dull.

When peers insisted that Haryana would never appreciate pure 70% dark couverture, advocating instead for cheap compound chocolates and heavy vegetable shortenings, Hardik doubled down. Ganachly became a statement: Faridabad and Delhi NCR deserved genuine, world-class artisanal indulgence without compromise.

Our Philosophy

THE 4 NON-NEGOTIABLE PILLARS

Every brownie, brookie, and ganache bar leaves our atelier bound by these four uncompromising rules of craft.

I
cookie
Pillar I

70% Dark Couverture (Never Compound)

Mainstream bakeries use industrial chocolate compound—a blend of cocoa powder and hydrogenated palm oil that coats the palate with wax. We insist on authentic couverture containing over 31% pure cocoa butter. It melts precisely at body temperature (36.5°C), delivering an immediate, intoxicating rush of deep fruity, earthy cacao notes.

II
science
Pillar II

100% Eggless Mastery

Removing eggs is not about substitution; it is about molecular reconstruction. Conventional baking relies on eggs for structure and moisture. We mastered water activity, fat emulsification, and slow gluten relaxation to yield a fudgy, dense core and a delicate crinkly top crust naturally—wholly cruelty-free and vegetarian.

III
eco
Pillar III

Zero Preservatives or Artificial Flavours

No potassium sorbate, no artificial vanilla essence, and zero shelf-life extending humectants. Real patisserie has a brief, honest life cycle. Our creations are formulated to be relished fresh within days of baking—the exact way grandmother recipes and Parisian confectioneries intended.

IV
bedtime
Pillar IV

Baked After 6:30 PM

By shifting our baking window strictly to twilight hours, our creations do not linger in display cases or dry storage. Ovens cool around 10:00 PM; parcels are rested overnight and dispatched by dedicated courier at dawn across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Faridabad.
