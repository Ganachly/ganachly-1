The Genesis

THE PURSUIT OF PURE CHOCOLATE

Ganachly wasn't conceived inside a venture-backed boardroom or an industrial commissary. It was forged in the quiet after-hours of Faridabad. By day, Hardik navigated the rigid demands of a full-time corporate profession; by twilight, the kitchen came alive. Every single batch was, and remains, crafted with care — things baked with love.

The name draws reverence from Ganache—that velvet alchemy of dark chocolate and fresh cream that serves as the beating pulse of traditional French baking. If the emulsion is rushed, it separates; if compromised with synthetic fillers, it goes dull.

While others focused on conventional approaches to chocolate making, Hardik envisioned something different — bringing the richness of pure 70% dark couverture and the art of premium baking to the region. He believed Faridabad and Delhi NCR deserved an elevated dessert experience, crafted with authentic ingredients, exceptional quality, and an uncompromising passion for artisanal indulgence. Ganachly was born from that vision.

Our Philosophy

THE 4 NON-NEGOTIABLE PILLARS

Every brownie, brookie, and ganache bar leaves our kitchen bound by these four uncompromising rules of craft.

I
cookie
Pillar I

70% Pure Dark Chocolate (Never Compound)

Mainstream bakeries use industrial chocolate compound—a blend of cocoa powder and hydrogenated palm oil that coats the palate with wax. We insist on authentic Belgian dark chocolate crafted with 100% pure cocoa butter. It melts precisely at body temperature (36.5°C), delivering an immediate, rich rush of deep cacao flavor.

II
science
Pillar II

100% Eggless Mastery

Removing eggs is not about substitution; it is about molecular reconstruction. Conventional baking relies on eggs for structure and moisture. We mastered water activity, fat emulsification, and slow gluten relaxation to yield a fudgy, dense core and a delicate crinkly top crust naturally—wholly cruelty-free and vegetarian.

III
eco
Pillar III

Zero Preservatives or Artificial Flavours

No potassium sorbate, no artificial vanilla essence, and zero shelf-life extending humectants. Real patisserie has a brief, honest life cycle. Our creations are formulated to be relished fresh within days of baking—the exact way grandmother recipes and Parisian confectioneries intended.

IV
bedtime
Pillar IV

Things Baked with Love

By shifting our baking window strictly to twilight hours, our creations do not linger in display cases or dry storage. Ovens cool around 10:00 PM; parcels are rested overnight and dispatched by dedicated courier at dawn across Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, and Faridabad.

Baking Rhythm

OUR JOURNEY TIMELINE

How your night-baked treats travel from our ovens to your doorstep.

01

Prep & Tempering

Callets of Belgian dark chocolate are melted and carefully hand-tempered for glossy shine and crisp snap.

02

First Oven Bake

Small pans hit the high-convection hearth. The kitchen fills with warm cocoa aroma as shiny crackled tops form.

03

Cooling & Slicing

Slow ambient resting to lock the fudgy center. Precise geometric cutting into Broken Bar sleeves.

04

Doorstep Dispatch

Sealed with care and packaged inside thermal sleeves for direct doorstep delivery in Faridabad.
